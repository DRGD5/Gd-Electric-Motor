Privacy Policy / Politiki y'ibanga

What we collect / Dukusanya iki

Name, phone number, email, orders, service requests and chat messages you send us, plus anonymous visit counts (a random ID stored in your browser).

Amazina, telefoni, email, komande, ubusabe bwa serivisi n'ubutumwa wohereza, hamwe n'umubare w'abasuye urubuga (utazwi uwo ari we).

Why / Impamvu

To process orders, contact you on WhatsApp or by phone, give support, and improve the website. We do not sell your data.

Ni ukugira ngo dutunganye komande, tuguhamagare cyangwa tukwandikire kuri WhatsApp, tukufashe, kandi tunoze urubuga. Ntitugurisha amakuru yawe.

Where it is stored / Aho abikwa

Google Firebase (accounts, orders, chat) and Cloudinary (photos). Only you and GD Electric Motor staff can see your orders and chats.

Your rights / Uburenganzira bwawe

You can ask us to correct or delete your account and data via WhatsApp or the contact on the website.

Ushobora gusaba ko amakuru yawe akosorwa cyangwa agasibwa ukatwandikira kuri WhatsApp.
